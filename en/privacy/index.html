4. Contact form, email and telephone

When you submit the contact form, your details are transmitted in encrypted form to an automation application (n8n): topic, language, email address and message, plus name and company if provided. To block automated submissions, a field invisible to people and the time between loading the form and submitting it are also transmitted. n8n and the NocoDB database run on a server operated by Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany.

The enquiry is stored in the database and forwarded to me by email. Email sending and the mailbox are provided by mailbox.org (Heinlein Hosting GmbH, Berlin).

You receive an automatic acknowledgement at the email address provided. In addition, the topic, language and message – not your name, company or email address – are sent to the Inference API of Hetzner Online GmbH, where a language model (Qwen) drafts my personal reply. According to Hetzner, the content of these requests is not stored. I receive the draft for review together with the topic, language, your name and the text of your message via the Telegram messenger service; your company and email address are not transmitted. Telegram may process this data outside the European Union. The reply is only sent after I have reviewed, if necessary edited and approved it; nothing is sent automatically without my approval. Enquiries recognised as advertising or automated are not answered and are deleted automatically after 30 days.

When you contact me by email or telephone, I process the information you provide, such as name, contact details, company and project context, to handle the enquiry and follow-up questions. Please avoid sensitive details that are unnecessary for an initial project enquiry.

Enquiries concerning a contract or pre-contractual measures are processed under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Other enquiries are processed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR based on the legitimate interest in effective communication.

Enquiries stored via the form and emails are deleted when the matter is concluded and there is no other reason to retain them. Statutory retention obligations remain applicable. The automation does not keep execution logs containing form content. Your data is not used for advertising without an appropriate legal basis.
