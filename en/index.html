What should I prepare?

A specific example from your working day and a list of the applications you use are enough for the first conversation. No sensitive client data is needed.

Who helps if something stops working?

I am your contact. Handover, defect resolution and any ongoing support are explicitly defined in the offer.

For IT projects

Do you join ongoing projects?

Yes. I support existing teams with development, modernization and integration. We agree on my role and responsibilities at the start.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

Primarily remotely. On-site collaboration in Baden-Württemberg and Southern Germany is possible by arrangement.

How does a modernization or AI project start?

With a conversation about the goal and existing systems. We agree on a limited scope and concrete outcome for a check; implementation is a separate engagement.

How do we handle sensitive data?

An initial description without confidential documents is enough. Before accessing data, we agree on confidentiality, permissions and suitable environments. The check does not require sending data to external AI services.
